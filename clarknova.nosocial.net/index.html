Desktop: Type with keyboard — keys light up. Shift = hold Shift | Lock = Shift+LCtrl | Enter = CR | BS = move left (no erase) | Tab = next stop | RCtrl = margin release | ↑↓ = roll paper | Scroll = zoom | Drag = pan
Mobile: Tap keys to type | Pinch = zoom | Drag = pan | Hold Shift + tap key = uppercase | ▲▼ = roll paper
Aim: A key only types when the press lands near its centre. A press on the key border, in a gap between keys, or off-centre is a miss and types nothing.
After a miss the keyboard pauses for a second, and every further tap restarts that pause — stop, wait, then carry on.
Touch guides
A small crosshair marks the exact spot you touched, so you can calibrate your aim: teal on a hit, pink on a miss. On a miss the pad turns pink and fades back to teal; taps during the pause only restart that fade.
Rules: Characters are permanent (ink). BS moves cursor back for overstriking. Bell at col 63. Margin stop at 71 — press MR to continue. Hard stop at 80 (prints in place). Reload = fresh sheet.
Pull the sheet:
Desktop — right-click the paper and choose Save image as…
Mobile (iOS/Android) — long-press the paper, then tap Save to Photos or Download image
Insert a page: Feed a previously pulled sheet, a form, or a card to type on top of it. The page must be 2480 × 3508 px (a sheet pulled from Clark Nova) — ±1 px tolerance for rounding.

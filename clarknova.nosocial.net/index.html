Desktop: Type with keyboard — keys light up. Shift = hold Shift | Lock = Shift+LCtrl | Enter = CR | BS = move left (no erase) | Tab = next stop | RCtrl = margin release | ↑↓ = roll paper | Scroll = zoom | Drag = pan
Mobile: Tap keys to type | Pinch = zoom | Drag = pan | Hold Shift + tap key = uppercase | ▲▼ = roll paper
Rules: Characters are permanent (ink). BS moves cursor back for overstriking. Bell at col 63. Margin stop at 71 — press MR to continue. Hard stop at 80 (prints in place). Reload = fresh sheet.
Aim: A key only types when the press lands near its centre. A press on the key border, in a gap between keys, or off-centre is a miss and types nothing.
After a miss the keyboard pauses for a second, and every further tap restarts that pause — stop, wait, then carry on.
Touch guides: A small crosshair marks the exact spot you touched, so you can calibrate your aim: teal on a hit, pink on a miss. On a miss the pad turns pink and fades back to teal; taps during the pause only restart that fade.
Paper feed: The two levers at the ends of the left roll bar are the paper path — the knobs between them still roll the platen.Top lever — pull a page in. Feed a previously pulled sheet, a form, or a card to type on top of it. The page must be 2480 × 3508 px (a sheet pulled from Clark Nova) — ±1 px tolerance for rounding.Bottom lever — pull the page out. Saves the sheet as a 2480 × 3508 PNG. If you pulled a page in, the name it came in under is offered again, so the sheet goes back where it came from.Paper goes in and out as an image. To turn a PDF into one, or a typed sheet back into a PDF, use PDFhero — pdfhero.nosocial.netReload for a fresh sheet.
The typist: The robot at the top of the right roll bar types a .txt file out on the machine for you — same strikes, same sounds, same keys lighting up, so the file comes off the platen as real typescript.
It keeps the carriage honest: lines wrap at the right margin, leading blanks are typed as TAB and spaces, and characters the machine has no slug for (brackets, dashes, smart quotes) are struck as the nearest thing it carries.
The keyboard is locked while it works and the pad turns grey. Press any key to take it back — that key stops the typist and types nothing. At the bottom of the page it stops on its own.
